## About

Robotics researcher currently pursuing a PhD at the [Humanoids and Human-Centered Mechatronics Lab (HHCM)](https://hhcm.iit.it/en-US/), Italian Institute of Technology, Genova, Italy.

Working on robot control for legged systems, focusing on reinforcement learning to address locomotion and loco-manipulation tasks.

M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/) (2024) and B.Sc. in Electronic and Computer Engineering from the [University of Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli"]() (2021), both cum laude.
